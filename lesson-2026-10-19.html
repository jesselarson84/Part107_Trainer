Basic Operating Limits

The core numbers of Part 107. These show up on the test constantly, usually with a trap answer next to them.

Mon, Oct 19~5 min read3 quiz questions

Altitude01

Maximum altitude is 400 ft AGL (above ground level).

Exception: when you're within 400 ft horizontally of a structure, you may fly up to 400 ft above its top. Move outside that 400-ft radius and the normal 400 ft AGL ceiling applies again.

Test trapAGL vs MSL. MSL is height above sea level. The limit is 400 ft AGL, never "400 ft MSL."

Speed02

Maximum groundspeed is 87 knots = 100 mph.

Test trapAnswers that swap the units, like "87 mph" or "100 knots." Both are wrong.

Visual line of sight03

You (or a visual observer) must keep the drone in sight with your own eyes the whole flight.

  • Glasses or contacts: OK
  • Binoculars, the camera feed, FPV goggles: don't count

Day, twilight & night05

☀DaylightFly as-is. No extra gear.
◐Civil twilight~30 min before sunrise / after sunset. Needs anti-collision lights.

Anti-collision lights must be visible for 3 SM

Other basics06

  • One aircraft at a time per remote pilot.
  • No flying from a moving vehicle, unless you're over a sparsely populated area.
  • Over people: separate rules, covered in a later lesson.
