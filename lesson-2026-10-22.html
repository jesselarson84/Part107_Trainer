Day 4 · Rules & Regulations

Operations Over People

When you're allowed to fly over someone, the four drone categories, crowds, and flying over moving cars.

Thu, Oct 22~5 min read3 quiz questions

The starting rule01

Default: you may not fly over a person. There are only three ways around that:

  • They're directly participating in the flight (like your visual observer).
  • They're under a covered structure or inside a stationary vehicle that protects them from a falling drone.
  • Your flight meets one of the four operational categories (section 02).
Test trap"OK if the person agrees" or "OK below 100 ft." Neither is a rule. Permission or a low altitude doesn't make a bystander a participant.

The four categories02

The idea: the less a drone could hurt someone, the easier it is to fly over people.

1Category 1Weighs 0.55 lb or less, everything on board included, and no exposed spinning parts that could cut skin.
2Category 2Impact no worse than 11 ft-lb of energy. Must be on an FAA-accepted declaration of compliance and labeled.
3Category 3Impact no worse than 25 ft-lb. Also declared and labeled, but with extra flight limits (section 04).
4Category 4Has an airworthiness certificate under Part 21 and follows its flight manual limits.

Lost label? If a Category 2 or 3 label is damaged or missing, the remote pilot must relabel it in English before flying over people.

Test trapCategory 1 isn't weight alone. A 0.5 lb drone with exposed props that could cut skin doesn't qualify.

Crowds (open-air assemblies)03

Cat 1, 2, 4sustained flight only with Remote ID
Cat 3never over a crowd
Quick passa brief, one-time transit isn't "sustained"

Remote ID compliant means a standard Remote ID drone or one with an FAA broadcast module.

Brief transit: crossing part of a crowd once, on the way somewhere unrelated, doesn't count as sustained flight over it.

Category 3's extra limits04

A Category 3 drone may fly over people only if one of these is true:

  • It's within or over a closed or restricted-access site, and everyone there is on notice a drone may fly over them.
  • It doesn't stay in sustained flight over anyone, except people who are participating or protected under cover.

Plus: never over open-air assemblies.

Over moving vehicles05

Not the same as yesterday: Day 3 was flying from a moving vehicle. This is flying over people inside moving cars.

Categories 1, 2, and 3: allowed only if the drone stays within or over a closed or restricted-access site with everyone in the vehicles on notice, or it doesn't keep sustained flight over moving vehicles.

Category 4: allowed if its flight manual limits don't prohibit it.

✓WaivableOperation over people (§107.39).
✓WaivableOperation over moving vehicles (§107.145).
!RememberSheltered and participating people never need a category.
