Registration, Marking & Remote ID

Which drones must be registered, where the number goes, and the three ways to meet Remote ID.

Fri, Oct 23~5 min read3 quiz questions

Who must register01

Part 107 rule: every drone you fly under Part 107 must be registered, no matter how light it is.

The only weight exception is for recreational flyers: a drone 0.55 lb or less, flown only for fun, doesn't need registering.

Part 107: each drone gets its own registration. Recreational: one registration covers all of the owner's hobby drones.

Where: online through FAADroneZone. Owners must be at least 13; a younger owner's drone is registered by someone 13 or older.

Test trap"Under 0.55 lb, no registration." That's only true for recreational flying. Take that same light drone on a paid job and it must be registered.

Marking the drone02

  • Put the registration number on an external surface of the drone.
  • It must be legible and stay attached for the whole flight.
  • Not inside the battery compartment, and not only on paperwork.

Paperwork: keep your registration certificate available when you fly, and show it if the FAA or law enforcement asks.

Test trapOlder guides say the number can go in the battery compartment. That changed in 2019. It must be on the outside now.

Remote ID: a digital license plate03

What it is: your drone broadcasts who it is and where it is, so people nearby can see it on a receiver like a phone.

Required since: September 16, 2023. You meet it one of three ways:

1StandardRemote ID built into the drone. Broadcasts the pilot's location.
2ModuleAn add-on broadcast module. Broadcasts the takeoff location.
3FRIANo Remote ID, but only inside an FAA-recognized identification area.

Remote ID rules to remember04

  • Takeoff to shutdown: it must broadcast the whole flight.
  • Before takeoff: make sure it's working. A standard Remote ID drone checks itself and won't take off if it fails.
  • Signal lost in flight: land as soon as practicable.
  • Serial number: the drone's or module's Remote ID serial number goes on its registration.
  • Never disable or tamper with Remote ID.

Module or FRIA: you must be able to see the drone the whole time.

Test trap"Keep flying, it's still registered." Registration doesn't replace Remote ID. If the broadcast stops, you land as soon as practicable.
